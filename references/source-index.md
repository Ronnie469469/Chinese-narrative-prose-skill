# Source Index

This index records the public basis used to pre-extract the style system. Runtime writing should rely on the extracted references, not on live source lookup.

## Research Boundary

Publicly available online material does not provide a complete legal full-text corpus of all winning works. For this skill, "all available works" means public book records, contents, official or media descriptions, event reports, and accessible commentary that can be used for high-level stylistic abstraction. Do not fabricate unavailable texts.

## Extracted Conclusions

- The style system should prioritize authenticity, individual expression, and flexible form.
- It should preserve youthful perception without encouraging uncontrolled confession.
- It should connect private experience to a wider world through concrete scenes.
- It should allow multiple submodes because the anthology tradition includes family, campus, love, city, social issues, experimental stories, and inward inquiry.
- It should actively resist formulaic school-essay endings and direct imitation of famous winners.
