# Revision Rubric

Use this rubric for self-checks, critiques, and rewrites.

## Scorecard

Score each dimension from 1 to 5 when the user asks for critique. For normal generation, use the dimensions silently and report only the most useful notes.

- **Material truth:** Does the prose feel grounded in credible lived detail?
- **Narrative movement:** Does something change beyond mood?
- **Scene density:** Are there enough concrete places, objects, actions, and sensory details?
- **Emotional restraint:** Does the essay delay, modulate, and earn emotion?
- **Language individuality:** Does the voice avoid both school-essay formula and ornamental imitation?
- **Ending resonance:** Does the ending return, open, or echo rather than conclude mechanically?

## Revision Actions

Use these actions in order:

1. **Anchor:** add or sharpen a concrete opening detail.
2. **Thread:** choose one recurring object, phrase, place, or action.
3. **Compress:** remove repeated direct explanations of emotion.
4. **Cool:** reduce adjectives when emotion is too loud.
5. **Warm:** add human gesture when prose feels abstract.
6. **Turn:** create a moment where the narrator understands differently.
7. **Echo:** revise the ending to recover an earlier detail.

## Common Diagnoses

- "只有情绪，没有事件": add a scene chain.
- "只有事件，没有认知": add a delayed recognition turn.
- "像作文题": remove explicit thesis statements and moral summaries.
- "像散句集": strengthen timeline, object thread, and paragraph transitions.
- "太用力": cut stacked metaphors, reduce grand nouns, let objects do more work.
- "太平": add contrast, withheld information, or a small reversal.
- "不像用户": replace generic literary persona with the user's actual places, speech, and details.

## Rewrite Rules

When rewriting a user's draft:

- Preserve factual claims unless the user asks for fictionalization.
- Preserve the core emotional relation even if structure changes.
- Do not make everyone sound more tragic than the material supports.
- Keep useful plain sentences; not every sentence needs literary polish.
- Replace imitated published-style phrasing with fresh, situation-specific language.

## Final Self-Check Format

For generated drafts, keep `风格自检` short:

- `叙事`: one note.
- `细节`: one note.
- `情绪`: one note.
- `语言`: one note.
- `可再加强`: one practical next revision.
